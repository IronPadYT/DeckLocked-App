-------------------------------------GUIDE FOR USING THE APP:-------------------------------------

Firstly click the person icon at the top. Type your profile name in and hit save.
This will retype your name into the text box as there is a bug which doesnt allow you to type in there once you have saved/loaded/deleted a profile.

When you create a new character make sure to increase Level to 2 by clicking the cog wheel icon and the + once. As when you draw cards it uses your level on the top for the requirements so this will allow you to match what you can unlock in game. Otherwise for example if you ding level 6 in game, it will still say level 5 in the app which means you cannot draw any of the new level 6 abilities.

Every time you draw a card you will need to click the card to unlock it. I done this as I needed to remake my account several times throughout the updating of the app and needed a way to keep drawing until I got the same cards as in my youtube series so I could continue it.

The cog wheel has some manual adjustment buttons in case things go wrong and you need to fix the values.

When you draw cards make sure to click them once they are drawn to ensure they are unlocked.


-----------------------------------------KNOWN BUGS -------------------------------------------

Whenever you save, load or delete a profile you can't type in the Profile name box. To get around this whenever you save a profile it auto types the
profile name into the box so you can save as many times as you want while progressing.

If you click gear or materials to unlock that slot, you can still pull those cards again as you need to "Pull" the cards to unlock them through the deck. so its best to make a new profile and keep drawing cards until you get the same unlocks if your profile bugs. This is why you can continuously reroll cards without using roll points as I was constantly updating the app I needed a way to remake the profiles. I do not reroll cards for the series however this is a debugging feature. I play with what rnjesus gives me.

If in doubt always close the app. Reopen and then load your profile before touching anything else in the app this should give it the best chance of loading everything correctly. I don't know how to code I just winged this

You have the ability to adjust the material roll values by +0.5 or -0.5 in case you accidently forgot to click a material card to unlock and cant roll as you only have 0.5 value left. Just remember to adjust it after you have fixed the roll.
